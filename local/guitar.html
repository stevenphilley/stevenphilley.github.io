Light notes on playing & gear

Acoustic vs electric

Acoustics reward quiet rooms and finger detail. Electrics need an amp (or a quiet interface) and ask different questions about tone, volume, and neighbors. Both belong on this shelf; neither needs a brand loyalty oath.

Brand-neutral practice

A setup that stays in tune, frets that don’t bite, and a strap that fits matter more than logo mythology. When a photo names Fender, Vox, or Stagg, that is attribution for the pictured instrument — not an endorsement or a claim about gear in Campbell.

South Bay context

This Local page sits next to Campbell weather, Dolphin Pet Village, and the DJ hobby starter. Public music-shop listings for San Jose / Campbell can go here later if they are sourced as ordinary public info — until then, no store is billed as “Steven’s spot.”
