RC time

A resistor and a capacitor set how fast a voltage rises or falls. Change R and C — τ updates as you type, with the familiar 63%, 95%, and 99% marks.

How it works

τ (tau) is the product of resistance and capacitance: τ = R × C. After one time constant, a charging capacitor reaches about 63% of the final voltage; after three, about 95%; after five, about 99%. Discharge follows the same marks toward zero.

Bigger R or C stretches the curve. Timing circuits, soft-start filters, and debounce networks all start from this product. The secondary figure 1 / (2πRC) is a rough corner-frequency scale for a simple RC low-pass — useful context, not a full filter design.
