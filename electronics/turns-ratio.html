Turns ratio

Primary and secondary turns set the voltage and current split. Ideal transformer math, plus mutual inductance from L1, L2, and k.

How it works

Ideal transformer: Vs / Vp = Ns / Np and Is / Ip = Np / Ns. The turns ratio a = Np / Ns steps voltage down (and current up) when a > 1.

Mutual inductance: M = k √(L1 L2), with |M| ≤ √(L1 L2) when 0 ≤ k ≤ 1. Perfect coupling is k = 1.
