Vol. Science · Astronomy · The spin flip

The 21-cm
hydrogen line

Neutral hydrogen in the ground state has two hyperfine levels. The electron and proton spins are parallel in the upper level and antiparallel in the lower one. The jump between them is a magnetic-dipole spin flip. Hellwig et al. 1970 give the unperturbed frequency as 1420.405751768 MHz, with a one-sigma uncertainty of 0.002 Hz. Divided into the exact speed of light, that frequency is a wavelength of 21.106114 cm, which rounds to 21.106 cm. The photon energy is 5.87433 µeV, which rounds to 5.874 µeV. hν/k is 0.068169 K, which rounds to 0.0682 K. The rounded figures are not substituted. This is not a Rydberg jump. Those optical and ultraviolet lines are the spectra desk.

Condon and Ransom print the Einstein A coefficient as A10 ≈ 2.85×10−15 s−1. The lifetime on this page is 1/A for that figure: 3.50877×1014 s, or 1.11186×107 Julian years of 31,557,600 s. About 1.1×107 years, and their 11 million years, are that number rounded. They are not substituted. An isolated atom almost never emits. The line is still observed because a galactic sightline contains an enormous number of atoms, and because collisions keep the upper level populated. The critical density is far below a typical interstellar density, so the atom does not have to wait out the radiative lifetime in isolation. At high redshift, Lyman-α scattering can do the same job. That is the Wouthuysen–Field coupling. This page does not compute it.

Cosmological redshift is the same definition as the redshift desk, f = 1420.405751768 / (1+z) MHz. At z = 0 the frequency rounds to 1420.406 MHz. At z = 17 it is 78.911 MHz, about 78.91 MHz. A radial velocity uses the radio convention, f = frest (1 − v/c), with recession positive. One km/s moves the line by 4.738 kHz, to 1420.401 MHz. The optical convention and the relativistic Doppler shift are printed beside it and are not substituted. For optically thin gas, NHI = 1.823×1018 cm−2 × ∫ TB dv, with the integral in K km/s. A Gaussian integral is 1.064467 × Tpeak × FWHM. 1.0645 is that factor rounded and is not substituted. Dust hides the optical Milky Way. This line does not. The speeds it measures, and the Keplerian fall they refuse, are the rotation-curve desk. The cooled blackbody behind the high-redshift absorption is the CMB desk.

Educational calculator. The rest frequency is Hellwig et al. 1970, 1420.405751768 MHz, uncertainty 0.002 Hz. The unrounded frequency is 1420.405751768 MHz. Rounded to 0.001 MHz it is 1,420.406 MHz. That rounding is not a second measurement. The wavelength is 21.106114 cm, which is 21.106 cm when rounded to 0.001 cm. The photon energy is 5.87433 µeV, which rounds to 5.874 µeV. hν/k is 0.068169 K, which rounds to 0.0682 K. The rounded figures are not substituted. The Einstein A coefficient used here is 2.85×10^−15 s^−1. The lifetime is 1/A: 3.50877×10^14 s, or 1.11186×10^7 Julian years of 31,557,600 s. About 1.1×10^7 years is that figure rounded. It is not substituted. Condon and Ransom’s 3.5×10^14 s and 11 million years are the same rounding. They are not substituted. What the line showed

Why a tiny rate is visible
A10 ≈ 2.85×10−15 s−1 means the mean spontaneous lifetime is 1.11186×107 years. Condon and Ransom note that the critical density is far below 1 cm−3, so collisions hold the transition in local thermodynamic equilibrium even in the outer disk of a spiral. In that limit three quarters of the atoms sit in the upper level, because the statistical weights are 3 and 1 and hν/k is only 0.068169 K. The collective emission of a long column is what a radio telescope records. The column calculator is that idea in the optically thin limit. It is not a named cloud.
Radio velocity
Radio astronomers defined v/c = (f_rest − f_obs) / f_rest because a spectrometer reads a frequency. Optical astronomers defined v/c = (λ_obs − λ_rest) / λ_rest = z, because a spectrograph reads a wavelength. The relativistic Doppler shift is a third number. At −300 km/s the three frequencies are 1,421.8271, 1,421.8286, and 1,421.8279 MHz. At 1 km/s the radio value is 1,420.401 MHz, 4.738 kHz below rest. The redshift desk prints c z and the relativistic β. This desk prints the radio convention those pages do not.
Andromeda and Virgo
SIMBAD’s heliocentric radial velocity for M31 is −300.0 ± 4.0 km/s, from McConnachie 2012. The redshift desk sets z = −0.001. This button sets −300 km/s in the radio convention. They are not the same frequency. Neither number is the Milky Way–Andromeda approach speed once the Sun’s motion is taken out. Virgo’s button is the round z = 0.004. Binggeli, Popescu, and Tammann 1993 give a mean heliocentric velocity of 1050 ± 35 km/s. c × 0.004 is 1,199.17 km/s. 0.004 is not their redshift.
Reionization and Cosmic Dawn
Planck 2018 results VI, Table 2, the same TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing column the CMB desk uses, gives a reionization midpoint z_re = 7.67 ± 0.73. The chart’s blue band, z = 6 to 10, is the usual teaching window around that midpoint. It is not a measurement of the edges. z = 6 is 202.915 MHz. z = 10 is 129.128 MHz. z = 17 is 78.911 MHz. Bowman et al. 2018 reported a profile centered at 78 MHz, which is z = 17.210 on this frequency. Hills et al. 2018 and Singh et al. 2022 (SARAS 3) dispute that profile. This page does not claim it.
Spiral arms
Ewen and Purcell detected the line in 1951, and Muller and Oort detected it in the same year. Dust blocks the optical plane of the Milky Way. The radio line passes through it. Oort, Kerr, and Westerhout 1958 used the Dutch and Australian surveys to draw the Galaxy as a spiral. The drawing is kinematic: a radial velocity plus an assumed rotation curve. Inside the solar circle the same velocity can sit at a near distance or a far one. The map is not a photograph of the arms.
Flat rotation curves
Beyond the solar circle that near-far ambiguity drops away, and the 21-cm speed can be read farther out than the bright starlight. A central point mass would fall as r−1/2. The outer speeds do not. The arithmetic of that fall is the rotation-curve desk. Its cited solar speed, 229.0 km/s from Eilers et al. 2019, is a stellar measurement. It is not a reduction of this line. Rubin and Ford 1970, the M31 result on that desk, used H II regions, not 21 cm.
