Approximate sky orientation by city or ZIP. Defaults to Campbell, CA (95008). Pick a month and local time; the wheel shows which major landmarks sit above the horizon from that viewpoint. Educational — approximate sidereal rotation, not a precision planetarium.

Method: local sidereal time ≈ Greenwich mean sidereal (Meeus-style approximation) + longitude. Horizon altitude from equitorial (RA/Dec) → alt/az. Bright-star catalog is a small public educational subset (Hipparcos/Yale Bright Star magnitudes, rounded). Place lookup via Open-Meteo geocoding. No paid APIs.
