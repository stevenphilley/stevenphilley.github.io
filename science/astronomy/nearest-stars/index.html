Nearest
stars

The stars within about a dozen light-years of Sol — our Sun. Distances are approximate (parallax / RECONS-style public values, rounded). Most neighbors are faint red dwarfs; Alpha Centauri is the famous bright exception.

Distance scale is linear in light-years (0–12 ly). Not a 3D map — just a readable yardstick. Spectral types use the standard OBAFGKM sequence.

Sol’s neighborhood

Tap a card or scale marker.

    • One light-year ≈ 9.46 trillion km — light’s travel in a Julian year.
    • Proxima Centauri is the closest known star; Alpha Cen A/B are a bright binary a hair farther.
    • Interstellar travel remains fiction at human timescales — Voyager 1 would need tens of thousands of years to reach even Proxima at current speed.
