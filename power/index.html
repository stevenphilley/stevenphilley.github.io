If I could do anything

Power

Not a manifesto. Just the honest answer to a simple question: if I had unlimited power for a while, what would I actually do with it? Start with the living world. Then keep walking toward the people and places I already care about.

Lead · Earth systems

01 · The Amazon

Keep the lungs living

Protect and restore the rainforest. Not as a slogan — as a place that still breathes for all of us. Stop the burning, grow the canopy back, and leave room for the people who already live with that forest.

02 · Clean air

Air you can trust

Sky that does not burn lungs. Cities and valleys where kids can play outside without checking an app first.

03 · Clean water

Water that stays good

Rivers, aquifers, and taps that stay drinkable. Less poison in the creeks. More care for the watersheds we all share.

04 · Natural life

Wild places, still wild

Creatures and ecosystems thriving — forests, coasts, deserts, and the quiet corridors between them. Not a museum. A living neighborhood for more than just us.

Then · Closer rings

Local place

Campbell · Bay Area

Care for the street-level world I walk — traffic that does not kill, sidewalks that feel like home, a town that still has a face. The same instinct as the big planet, just closer. See the local desk.

Animals & pets

Kindness with a heartbeat

Animals that share our blocks and homes deserve gentle rooms and patient hands. I’d amplify the everyday kindness around places like Dolphin Pet Village — not own a zoo, just make more room for soft landings.

Family & friends

Time, not just gifts

Power that buys back evenings. Longer tables. Calls that are not rushed. The album on the family page is the point — people, together, while we still can.

Making & teaching

Bench tools that teach

Electronics that help people understand circuits — ohms you can feel, LEDs that light for a reason. Playful making too: games and creative software in the Color It Company spirit. Start at the electronics desk.

Music

DJ & guitar, turned up

Joy I’d amplify — decks that move a room, a guitar that stays in reach. Not fame. More nights where music is the reason people show up. Notes live under DJ and guitar.

Seeing & sharing

Look, then pass it on

Photography, a public photos library, and the habit of curiosity — science headlines, coast light, the weird beautiful planet. Seeing clearly is part of caring. Browse the portfolio and public photos.

I’d use power for life, not spectacle. Leave the fireworks. Keep the forest, the air, the water, the animals, the town, the people, the bench, the song. This page is a draft of that wish — rewrite any line whenever the wish gets clearer.
