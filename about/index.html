About

This is Steven Philley’s personal site — a home for tools, a science desk, local Campbell notes, photography links, and small experiments.

Nothing here pretends to be a company homepage. It’s one person’s desk on the web: calculators and benches, headlines worth keeping, street-level notes from Campbell and the Bay Area, and links out to the photos.

Everywhere else and the socials live on Linktree. For the longer wish list — what he’d do if he could do anything — see Power.

Public lines from Facebook / Color It Game Studio live on Quotes — filled only from text that is publicly visible.
