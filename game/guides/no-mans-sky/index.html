Ellipses and dots stand in for the disk. This is not a star catalog. Click a base or a system mark to open the inventory, mining, and crops pinned there. Pink circles are your planetary bases. Cyan diamonds are Euclid Hub references. Stars are quadrant references; the name shows when you point at one or choose it in the list. The cross is the galactic center. Scroll or pinch to zoom toward the pointer. Zoom in and Zoom out follow the base under the pointer, or the selected base, and that base stays put as the scale changes. Bases in the same system spread apart as you zoom; a count sits on the pile until they separate, then their names show. Drag to pan. Reset view fits your bases and the Hub marks. Double-click a base, or click it in the list, to bring that base to the middle.

    Quadrant references

    About five community landmarks in each Euclid quadrant, plus the four Polaris systems at the Fade. Stars, not the Hub diamonds. The Hub capital, HUB1, and the former capital stay on the Hub layer, so they are not drawn again here. Gamma’s civilization core is those Hub marks. AGT is the extra Gamma hub in Yihelli; its embassy (SSI 005F) sits in the same region as the Firstfall address below.

      Coordinates and civilizations are from the NMS Hub and Civilized space pages on the No Man's Sky Wiki, the Polaris articles linked from The Fade, and the Galactic Hub Project page on Miraheze. These are community landmarks. Capitals move, and a directory row can be older than the civilization page. Wiki “:PC” on a Polaris address is plotted as system 0001. Glyphs use planet index 1, so the first glyph can differ from a wiki address that names another planet in the same system. The map position does not. This disk is a schematic, not a catalog.

      How an address is read

      Each base’s packed GalacticAddress is a 48-bit portal code. Extra high bits are ignored. The bits are planet, system, and voxel Y, Z, and X — the twelve portal glyphs, P-SSS-YY-ZZZ-XXX. This map does not invent a galaxy index from that integer. When the save includes a separate RealityIndex, the galaxy menu uses that. Signal-booster coordinates add the usual offsets: X and Z +0x7FF, Y +0x7F. Distance uses 400 light-years per voxel, measured from the center. Exported JSON is read as-is. A Steam .hg save is decompressed here, so that file does not need NomNom first. Obfuscated keys are renamed with a local key map. Nothing is uploaded.
