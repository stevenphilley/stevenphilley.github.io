Sources

Charging carbon, sodium, or oxygen into a suit or ship is fuel. It is not one of these rows. Basic mineral ratios follow the Waypoint and Endurance articles on the No Man's Sky Wiki (Miraheze). Salt, the atmospheric gases, di-hydrogen, the asteroid metals, the plant burns, and the craft blueprints follow those item pages, some as recent as Worlds Part II. Three-input large-refiner recipes are left out, including alloy lattices and the warp-cell bypass. Frost Crystal is left out: the Carbon article does not list it under Burn Plantlife. Cooking is left out. A later patch can change a recipe. Add an edge as another object in minerals.json.
