Vol. VI — Guides

Refine

An edge explorer for the shared material graph. Pick an item. Made from is what produces it. Converts to is what spends it, in a refiner or in the inventory. A badge marks the route: 1 Portable, 2 Medium+, or Inventory. The address carries the item. Ratios follow the Miraheze wiki. A later patch can change them.

The moodboard is the selection surface. Pins live there. Logistics turns a recipe into a build cost. The base map shows where a hold sits.

The ladders: ferrite, carbon, sodium, cobalt, the stellar metals into chromatic metal, salt into chlorine, and tritium into di-hydrogen. A double stroke means the pair converts both ways somewhere in this set. Plants, earth elements, gases beyond oxygen, and crafted pieces stay in the list. Oxygen rows marked Expansion multiply a stock. The panel carries the ratios.

Sources

Charging a technology is fuel. It is not a refiner recipe and it is not a craft row. Craft rows are made in station inventory. Wiring loom is bought, not crafted, so it has no edge. High-speed sublimation is the three-slot path. Other three-input large-refiner rows are left out.
