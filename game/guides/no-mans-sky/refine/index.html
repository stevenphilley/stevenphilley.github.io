Ferrite, carbon, sodium, cobalt, and the stellar metals into chromatic metal. A double stroke means the pair converts both ways somewhere in this set, including recipes that need a second input. Oxygen rows marked Expansion multiply a stock. The panels above carry the ratios.

Sources

Charging carbon, sodium, or oxygen into a suit or ship is fuel. It is not one of these rows. The ratios follow the No Man's Sky Wiki on Miraheze, from the Waypoint and Endurance articles for these items. Silver to paraffinium is left out, because silver is not in this set. Activated metals, quartzite, plants, and atmospheric gases are left out too. A later patch can change a recipe. Add an edge as another object in minerals.json.
