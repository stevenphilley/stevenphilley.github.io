Game guides opens at /game/guides/, separate from the playable /games/ pages. First tool: a No Man's Sky base map. Tides desk

Astronomy grows a tides page: a top-down Earth with exaggerated ocean bulges. Slide the Moon from new to full and the label switches from spring to neap. A light spin shows two highs per turn under bulges that stay put. Cited NASA and NOAA. Schematic, not a tide table — and not a Campbell harbor guide.

Eclipses desk

Astronomy grows an eclipses page: a side-view Sun, Earth, and Moon on a tilted orbit. Toggle solar or lunar, then slide the node offset from aligned to about 5° and watch the shadow miss. Umbra, penumbra, nodes, eclipse seasons. Cited NASA. Schematic, not a prediction.

The seasons desk adds day length by latitude — a slider from pole to pole, tied to the same day-of-year control. Near equinox the strip stays near 12 hours; solstice stretches toward the poles. Classroom hour-angle math, not a sunrise almanac.
