Tools

Eight pages
02
Annual Dial circular-calendar.html

A calendar built like a clock. Read the whole year as one engraved dial, or switch to a single month with live hands. Click any day to mark it.

 03
MarketPulse markets/marketpulse.html

A finance terminal. Live crypto prices from CoinGecko with a scrolling ticker, index snapshots, and business headlines pulled from seven feeds.

 04
Percentages markets/percent-calculator.html

Three kinds of percentage math in one place — what a percent of a number is, what one number is as a percent of another, and the change between two figures. Results update as you type.

 05
Pomodoro pomodoro.html

A focus timer with three sessions — pomodoro, short break, long break — each with its own color. Set your own durations, and a chime marks the end of every round.

 06
Stilltype typing-tutor.html

A quiet typing tutor. Three difficulty levels, a beginner mode that drills the keys you choose, and a pace chart with words per minute and accuracy at the end of every run.

 07
Nine Chakras chakras/index.html

The nine chakras of the tantric subtle body — name, meaning, element, seed sound, and the qualities each governs — with a sticky scroll-spy rail and petal marks drawn to scale.

 08
Tools desk tools/index.html

Everyday converters hub under /tools/ — temperature first, more to come. Runs in the browser.

 09
Temperature tools/temperature.html

Fahrenheit ↔ Celsius, both ways. Type either side; results update live. Freezing, room, body, and boiling presets.

Electronics

Ten pages
E0
Electronics electronics/index.html

Watts to amps, amps to watts, watts to volts, Ohm's law, and power from resistance. Runs in the browser.

 E1
Circuit explorer electronics/circuits.html

Beginner interactive trainer — 10 lessons. Close a loop, Ohm's law, series, parallel, LEDs, capacitors, RC time, and voltage dividers.

 E2
Resistor codes electronics/resistor-codes.html

Interactive 4-band color decoder. Click the bands or type ohms to snap to a nearby E24 value.

 E3
LED resistor electronics/led-resistor.html

Series resistor for an LED from supply volts, forward voltage, and milliamps. Nearest E24 and resistor power.

 E4
Voltage divider electronics/voltage-divider.html

Two-resistor divider: Vin, R1, and R2 in; Vout, current, and power in each resistor. Runs in the browser.

 E5
Series & parallel electronics/series-parallel.html

Combine two to four resistors in series or parallel. Live Req and nearest E24.

 E6
Current divider electronics/current-divider.html

Two parallel resistors share a source current. Iin, R1, and R2 in; branch currents, voltage, Req, and power out.

 E7
RC time electronics/rc-time.html

RC time constant from R and C. τ, plus 1τ / 3τ / 5τ charge marks and a 1/(2πRC) corner scale.

 E8
Reactance electronics/reactance.html

AC reactance from frequency and C or L. Xc or Xl, plus |Z| and phase with series R.

 E9
RL time electronics/rl-time.html

RL time constant from R and L. τ = L / R, plus 1τ / 3τ / 5τ current marks and a R/(2πL) corner scale.

 E10
LC resonance electronics/resonance.html

LC resonant frequency from L and C. f₀ = 1/(2π√(LC)), plus ω₀, Z₀, and series Q / bandwidth when R > 0.

 E11
Capacitor codes electronics/capacitor-codes.html

EIA 3-digit capacitor marking decoder. Read pF / nF / µF from the code, or look up a value with E12 snap.
