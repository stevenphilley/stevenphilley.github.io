Site-wide

sp-theme
Global · photography, local, weather, tools, electronics, power, publicphotos, sitemap…

Dusk, Sage, Tide, and Sand share one localStorage key: sp-theme. Choosing a card or header swatch sets html[data-theme] and persists across pages that already listen for it.

Scoped families

Do not merge

These palettes stay on their own routes. Opening them does not rewrite sp-theme, and this hub will not apply them site-wide.
