Unit circle trainer

Five drills. Angle to point: read the angle, choose (cos, sin). Point to angle: name the highlighted spot. Convert: degrees and radians, both ways. Function: exact sin, cos, or tan. Tap: click that spot on the circle.

Keys 1–4 pick an answer. On the circle, Tab or the arrow keys move between spots and Enter confirms.

Quadrant I is 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°. All quadrants is the full 16.

A miss shows the reference angle, the quadrant sign — All Students Take Calculus: I all positive, II sine, III tangent, IV cosine — and the 1-2-3 pattern √1/2, √2/2, √3/2 for sine of 30°, 45°, 60°.

Cards you miss come back more often. Streak, accuracy, and the 60-second best stay in this browser.
