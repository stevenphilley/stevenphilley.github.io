Common angles

Remember

SOH · CAH · TOA — opposite/hypotenuse, adjacent/hypotenuse, opposite/adjacent in a right triangle. On the unit circle, hypotenuse is 1, so sin = y and cos = x.

Check yourself

A short SOHCAHTOA / identity check. Answers use exact common values where possible.

Useful identities

Pythagorean: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 (always true on the unit circle — watch the live check in the readout).

Tangent: tan θ = sin θ / cos θ when cos θ ≠ 0.

Cofunction: sin(90° − θ) = cos θ, and cos(90° − θ) = sin θ (in degrees).
