Desk map

Three lanes · open any card

Start with trig if you want angle, sin/cos, and SOHCAHTOA in your fingers. Linear algebra if you want matrices and vectors on a plane. Calculus when you are ready for rates of change, areas under curves, and limit intuition.

Trigonometry
Unit circle · SOHCAHTOA

Click or drag the angle. Live sin, cos, tan. Degree/radian toggle, common-angle presets, and a short identity / SOHCAHTOA check.
Linear algebra
Matrices · vectors · eigenvalues

2×2 multiply with a visual, determinant and inverse when it exists, 2D vector add/scale on a canvas, and an optional eigenvalue stretch.
Calculus
Derivative · integral · limit

Tangent line on a selectable f(x), Riemann-sum slider for a definite integral, and a limit-intuition demo as x approaches a point.

How to use

  • Touch and mouse. Canvases accept drag and tap; controls are large enough for phones.
  • Read the readout. Numbers update live — treat them as the worked example next to the picture.
  • Change the inputs. Presets and sliders are there so you can ask “what if” without rewriting the page.
  • Nearby desks. Pair with Science, Electronics, and the site map when you want applications.
