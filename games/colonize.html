How to play

End turn to harvest and move the rival. Explorers reveal fog (3-tile vision). Colonies need land (not water). Farms→food, mines→ore (best on hills), labs→research toward Charter win.

Win / lose. Hold 5 colonies or reach 100% charter research. Lose if you have 0 colonies. Original light strategy — not based on any commercial 4X title.
