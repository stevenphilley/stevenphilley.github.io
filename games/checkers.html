Checkers

English / American rules on an 8×8 board. Click or tap a piece, then a highlighted square. Forced captures are enforced.

Rules: Pieces move diagonally on dark squares only. Men move forward; kings move either way. Captures are mandatory when available (including multi-jumps). Crown on the far rank. Win when the opponent has no pieces or no legal moves. Computer plays Black with a greedy capture preference, otherwise a random legal move.
