The Winds Of Evening

Wilbur Wood Philley

As the sun slides lazily o’er the hill
   And the day goes slowly to rest,
The soft winds of evening gently thrills
    Your heart with its loneliness.

  It carries you to some distant land
      Where people know no fears;
  And where life’s love and happiness
     Goes on for years and years.

As the cool wind soothes your throbbing head
 And brings hope to your aching soul,
It heightens your spirits, lifts up your heart;
   And helps you to seek your goal.

You stand with your face dividing the breeze;
   You strengthen your heart again,
To face tomorrow’s soul breaking trials;
     And bear the mistakes of men.
