How it counts

  • Characters include spaces and line breaks. “No spaces” drops any Unicode whitespace.
  • Words are runs of non-space. CJK and similar scripts will under-count — this is a Latin-ish desk.
  • Sentences split on . ? ! …. Common abbreviations (Mr., Dr., U.S.) are skipped when obvious. Unfinished last sentences still count.
  • Lines follow the textarea (empty draft = 0). Paragraphs are blocks split by a blank line.
  • Bytes are UTF-8. Emoji and accented letters cost more than one byte.
  • Reading time is ~230 words per minute — a casual adult skim, not a lab measure.
  • The limit is soft. Going over tints the box; it does not block typing.

Privacy

  • Everything runs in this tab. There is no form post and no analytics on the draft.
  • Copy uses the clipboard API. Download builds a .txt in the browser.
  • The soft-limit choice can stay in localStorage. The text itself is not saved.
