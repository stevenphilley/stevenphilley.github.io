Speed test

Latency, download, and upload from this browser to Cloudflare’s public speed-test servers. That is browser-to-test-server throughput — not a promise of your ISP’s line rate. Press Start; nothing runs on its own.

Not available offline. This page needs a live network to reach the test servers.

Standard uses about 25 MB down and 8 MB up of generated test data. Light is about 4.5 MB down and 2 MB up — better on phones or metered links. Neither sends your files.

Privacy & method

The test generates random-looking binary blobs in this tab. It does not read your disk or upload personal files. Endpoints still see your IP address, user-agent, and timing — the same as any web request.

Latency is the median of several tiny cache-busted GETs. Jitter is the mean absolute gap between consecutive samples. Download times one or more cache-busted GETs. Upload POSTs generated blobs; if that is blocked, the card says unavailable instead of inventing a number.

Servers: speed.cloudflare.com/__down and __up — Cloudflare’s public speed-test endpoints, CORS-open. Results move with Wi-Fi, device, VPN, browser tabs, and how busy that edge is. A failed stage keeps whatever already finished.
